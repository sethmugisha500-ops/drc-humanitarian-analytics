# KPI Definitions

**People in Need:** sum of assessed people in need.

**People Reached:** sum of people reached through response records.

**Overall Coverage:** people reached divided by people in need.

**Funding Gap:** funding requirements minus funding received, floored at zero for management reporting.

**Funding Coverage:** funding received divided by requirements.

**Active Interventions:** sum of intervention counts represented in response records.

**Priority Index:** composite 0–100 score combining need pressure, severity and low response coverage. It is a portfolio methodology, not an official humanitarian prioritization standard.
